# Prerequisites

You will need an IBM Cloud account to gain access to this demonstration environment.
You will need an IBM account and corresponding IBMid to gain access to IBM Technology Zone.
In addition, you must be familiar with the Linux operating system, as well as know how to use the command line interface to execute basic Linux commands such as kubectl, oc, exit, ls, mkdir, ssh, sudo, and curl (to name few). Ideally, you should also have some experience executing Red Hat OpenShift Container Platform and Kubernetes command line interface (CLI) commands. If you need experience using Linux, Kubernetes and Red Hat OpenShift Container Platform, it is highly recommended that you complete the following Red Hat courses before you attempt to work through the exercises in this lab.
Red Hat Courses:
- [DO080 – Containers, Kubernetes and Red Hat OpenShift Technical Overview](https://training-lms.redhat.com/sso/saml/auth/rhopen?RelayState=deeplinkoffering%3D43022363) ([https://training-lms.redhat.com/sso/saml/auth/rhopen?RelayState=deeplinkoffering%3D43022363](https://training-lms.redhat.com/sso/saml/auth/rhopen?RelayState=deeplinkoffering%3D43022363))
- [DO180 – Red Hat OpenShift Administration I: Managing Containers and Kubernetes](https://training-lms.redhat.com/sso/saml/auth/rhopen?RelayState=deeplinkoffering%3D46105986) ([https://training-lms.redhat.com/sso/saml/auth/rhopen?RelayState=deeplinkoffering%3D46105986](https://training-lms.redhat.com/sso/saml/auth/rhopen?RelayState=deeplinkoffering%3D46105986))

## Create an account to request your environment
IBM employees can use their IBM credentials to access IBM Technology Zone or IBM Cloud.
Business Partners must use an IBMid for IBM Technology Zone. If you do not have an IBMid, you can create one by using steps in this document. There is no cost to creating an IBMid.
You might also require an IBM Cloud account for some lab environments.
Additionally, you might need to configure an OpenVPN connection to be able to access the lab environment.
### Creating an IBMid
1. In your web browser, go to the IBM account page (https://www.ibm.com/account/us-en/).
1. Click **Log in to My IBM** (A).

![Screenshot]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/image3.png)

1. The Log in to IBM page is displayed. Click **Create an ****IBMid** (A).

![Screenshot]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/image4.png)

1. On the **Create an ****IBMid** page, enter the following information:
- Your email address (A)
- A password for your IBMid account (B)
- First name (C)
- Last name (D)
- Your country or region of residence (E) and state or province (F)
- If you select that you are not a student (G), then enter the name of your company (G)
- Click **Next** (I) to continue

![Screenshot]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/image5.png)

1. You are then prompted to verify your email and complete your account creation. Enter the code that was emailed to you in **Verification token**** (A)**, and then click **Create account**** (B)**.

![Screenshot]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/image6.png)

1. The **About your ****IBMid**** Account** page is shown. Click **Proceed**** (A)**.

![Screenshot]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/image7.png)

1. After a few minutes, you will receive an email confirming that your IBMid has been activated.
> Note: The first time that you log in using your IBMid you will be sent another code to verify your log in.
