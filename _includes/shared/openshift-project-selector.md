The Project selector is used to switch between the different OpenShift projects available. To switch to a specific project, click on the **Project **Selector** drop-down list** (A) and then click on the appropriate project name shown in the **Projects list** (B). You can limit the list of projects shown OR search for a specific project by entering search criteria in the **Search entry field** (C). You can also create a new project by clicking on the **Create **Project** button** (D) shown at the bottom of the screen.

> **Note:** The **Show **default projects** toggle switch** (E) will show/hide projects that start with the name `openshift-`.
